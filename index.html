Daybloom privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Daybloom does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

Everything you create in Daybloom (your moments, dates, flower choices and settings) is stored only on your device. Daybloom has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, Daybloom asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Backups

When you back up your garden, Daybloom writes a file to a location you choose. Daybloom never sends that file anywhere.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Daybloom removes all of its data from your device.
