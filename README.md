# Daybloom privacy policy

The privacy policy for [Daybloom: Countdown Garden](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nthnpy3.daybloom), an Android app that turns the moments you're waiting for into flowers that grow.

Live page: https://n1th1n-19.github.io/daybloom-privacy/

In short: Daybloom has no account, no ads, no analytics and no network access. Everything stays on your device.

Questions: support@daystack.space
